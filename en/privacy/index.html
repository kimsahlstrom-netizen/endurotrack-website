Privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026

EnduroTrack is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that records enduro, motocross, snowmobile and ATV rides, shows statistics and lets you follow other riders. This policy explains which personal data we process, why, for how long, and your rights.

Kim Sahlström (private individual), Sweden Email: support@endurotrack.se

We do not collect data for advertising, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell personal data.

Apple Health: EnduroTrack reads workouts, heart rate, calories and routes from Apple Health and saves rides there, only with your permission in iOS. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

You can withdraw any consent at any time under More → Privacy & Consent. Withdrawing health data consent deletes your heart rate and calories from your account.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other signed-in users see your profile (name, username, bio, profile picture).
  • Rides and statistics follow your settings: only you, followers, or all signed-in users. With a private account you approve new followers.
  • GPS routes are shown to others only if you share the route of that ride. Points inside your privacy zones are always hidden from others.
  • Heart rate and calories are shown to others only if you turn sharing on.
  • Blocked users cannot see your rides, and you cannot see theirs.

5. Processors and transfers

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) – database, sign-in, profile picture storage. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland). Supabase is a US company; any access from outside the EU/EEA is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under Supabase's data processing agreement.
  • Resend (Resend Inc.) – delivers account emails such as email confirmation and password reset. Resend processes your email address and the message itself. Resend is a US company; the transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under Resend's data processing agreement.
  • Apple – Apple Health/HealthKit on your device and Apple Maps for map display, under Apple's own privacy policy.

6. Retention

  • As long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account (More → Delete account), your profile, profile picture, rides, routes, health data, zones, consents, follows, likes, comments, blocks and reports are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • Security logs at our hosting provider are kept for a limited time according to the provider's routines.
  • Apple Health data is not affected by deleting your EnduroTrack account; manage it in the Health app.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data – export it yourself under More → Privacy & Consent → Export my data,
  • rectify inaccurate data (e.g. in Edit Profile),
  • erasure (delete your account in the app),
  • restrict or object to certain processing,
  • data portability (the export is a machine-readable file),
  • withdraw consent at any time.

Contact support@endurotrack.se. We reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), www.imy.se.

8. Security

Measures include encrypted transmission, database access rules limiting who can read what, private GPS routes by default, privacy zones, and health data stored only with consent. No service can guarantee absolute security; please protect your password and devices.

9. Children

EnduroTrack is not intended for children under 13.

10. Changes

We will inform you in the app about significant changes. The current version is always available at https://endurotrack.se/en/privacy/ and in the app.
