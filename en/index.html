Built for riders

Start on your wrist

GPS, heart rate, laps and pause right on Apple Watch, Wear OS or Garmin. Leave your phone in the van – the ride shows up in the app afterwards.

Get Maxvarv for Garmin →

Race your friends

Follow your riding friends, give a Ride On and see who rode the most this week, month or year.

Your route. Your rules.

Routes are private by default. Privacy zones hide where you live. Heart rate is only stored with your consent. No cross-app tracking, we never sell data, and everything is stored in the EU.

Lap times and PBs

Ride over the track's start/finish and laps are counted automatically. See your track records, get "NEW PB!" when you beat them and compare laps with friends.

Garage and suspension

Engine hours are counted from your rides. Log service, tyres and receipts, measure sag and save suspension settings per track.

Heart rate your way

Connect a heart rate strap straight to your phone – iPhone or Android – or ride with Apple Watch, Wear OS, Garmin or Polar.
